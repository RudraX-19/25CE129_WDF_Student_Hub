Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to StudentHub?

Go to the Login page, enter your registered Student ID or email and password, then click Log In.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Contact the administration office via the Contact page and they will reset your credentials.

How do I register as a new student?

Visit the Registration page, fill in all required details, and click Create Account. After registration, log in using your new credentials.

Where can I view my grades?

Your grades are available on the Grades & Transcripts page accessible from the navigation menu after login.

How do I register for campus events?

Browse upcoming events on the Events & Clubs page and click the "Register Now" link for your desired event.

How do I provide feedback?

Use the Feedback page to share your suggestions or report issues with the portal.
