About StudentHub

Our Mission

StudentHub is a centralized academic portal designed to simplify student life by bringing all academic resources, schedules, grades, and campus activities into one unified platform.

Our Vision

To empower every student with seamless access to the tools and information they need to succeed academically and beyond.

Key Features

  • Centralized academic dashboard
  • Real-time timetable and schedule management
  • Grade tracking and transcript access
  • Campus events and club directory
  • Digital library resources
  • Faculty announcements and notices
